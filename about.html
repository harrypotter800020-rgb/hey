About MediConnect

Student project – CV Raman Global University

Our Story

MediConnect is a purpose-driven healthcare accessibility project, born from the vision of five passionate 3rd-year students at CV Raman Global University, Bhubaneswar. Developed as our major 6th-semester project under the valuable guidance of Ipsita Ankita Hota, MediConnect aims to bridge the gap between people and essential medical services using simple, accessible technology.

We observed that in critical moments, finding immediate help or reliable health information can be overwhelming. MediConnect is our solution—a mobile-friendly web app designed to put essential healthcare services and information right at your fingertips.

Meet the team

Key Features

10km Medical Finder
Emergency Call Dialer
Disease & Medicine details
Medicine Reminders
Consult & Blog
One‑tap navigation

The app auto‑detects your location to show nearby hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies. It also includes a comprehensive health encyclopedia, emergency numbers for India, and daily reminder notifications.
